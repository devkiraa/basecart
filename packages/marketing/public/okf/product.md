# Basecart — Product Overview

Basecart is an e-commerce platform built for Indian sellers: brands, Instagram boutiques, home bakers, and WhatsApp-based sellers. Merchants launch an online store in about two minutes with no code.

## Who it serves

- Indian D2C brands and boutiques
- Instagram sellers converting DM orders into structured checkouts
- WhatsApp-order businesses (bakers, saree designers, clothing boutiques)
- Local sellers across Kerala and India

## Core value proposition

- 0% platform transaction fees on every paid plan
- Native WhatsApp checkout and order alerts
- Instant UPI & COD payments via Razorpay
- Shiprocket / Delhivery / DTDC / Speed Post shipping automation
- Isolated per-tenant databases (security and performance)
- 3-month free trial, no credit card required
