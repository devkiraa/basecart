# Basecart Competitor Comparisons

## Basecart vs Shopify

- Basecart: 0% platform transaction fees; no app-store subscription stack.
- Shopify: charges transaction fees unless using Shopify Payments; paid app ecosystem adds monthly costs.
- Basecart pricing: ₹99–₹2,999/month with 3-month free trial.
- Built for Indian sellers: UPI, WhatsApp checkout, regional shipping.

## Basecart vs WooCommerce

- WooCommerce is self-hosted: you pay for hosting, plugins, and maintenance; performance depends on your stack.
- Basecart is fully managed: hosting, SSL, and performance included; isolated per-tenant databases.
- Basecart includes WhatsApp checkout and UPI/COD out of the box.

## Basecart vs Meesho

- Meesho is a marketplace for resellers; Basecart is a storefront platform where you own your brand and domain.
- Basecart: custom domains, 0% platform fees, direct customer relationships.
