# Basecart Pricing

All prices in INR (₹), billed monthly. Basecart charges 0% platform transaction fees on every paid plan — merchants pay only standard payment gateway MDR.

## Free Trial

- Price: ₹0/month
- Duration: 3 months, or first 1,000 orders / ₹25,000 in sales — whichever comes first
- Includes: full Growth-plan features
- No credit card required. Cancel anytime.

## Basic — ₹99/month

- 100 product listings
- Direct WhatsApp checkout
- Razorpay & Stripe payment gateways
- Manual UPI & COD tracking
- Standard storefront theme

## Plus — ₹499/month

- Custom domain mapping (yourbrand.com)
- 500 product listings
- Custom storefront themes
- WhatsApp & email notifications

## Growth — ₹1,499/month (Recommended)

- Unlimited products & orders
- Shiprocket automated shipping & AWBs
- Abandoned-cart recovery (WhatsApp & email)
- AI description writer & marketing tools
- Product options matrix (sizes, colors)

## Business — ₹2,999/month

- 5 staff seats
- Developer REST API & custom webhooks
- Custom CSS/JS injection
- Automated GST invoices & PDF export
- Dedicated account manager & 24/7 support

## Policy notes

- No per-order platform fees on any plan.
- WhatsApp checkout, abandoned-cart recovery, Shiprocket automation, and the AI writer are built in — no per-app subscription stack.
- Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from the dashboard.
