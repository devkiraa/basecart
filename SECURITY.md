# Security Policy

Basecart takes the security of our platform, merchant data, and customer transactions very seriously. We appreciate the work of security researchers and developers in keeping our community safe.

---

## Supported Versions

Only the latest active version deployed on the `master` / `main` branch receives active security updates and vulnerability patches.

| Version | Supported |
| :--- | :--- |
| `1.x.x` (Latest / Master) | :white_check_mark: |
| `< 1.0.0` | :x: |

---

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability or potential threat in Basecart, please report it to our security team as soon as possible.

### 📧 How to Report
- **Email**: `security@basecart.app`
- **PGP Key / Security Page**: [https://basecart.app/.well-known/security.txt](https://basecart.app/.well-known/security.txt)

### 📋 What to Include in Your Report
1. **Description**: Clear summary of the vulnerability (e.g. XSS, CSRF, Access Control, Authentication bypass).
2. **Proof of Concept (PoC)**: Step-by-step instructions or HTTP requests to reproduce the issue.
3. **Impact**: Potential impact on merchants or customer data.

### ⏱️ Response Time & SLA
- **Initial Acknowledgment**: Within 24 hours.
- **Triage & Risk Assessment**: Within 48 hours.
- **Fix & Deployment**: Emergency patches for Critical issues are deployed within 24 hours.

---

## Disclosure Policy

- Please do **not** disclose the issue publicly until our security team has addressed and released a patch.
- We request that you avoid privacy violations, destruction of data, and interruption or degradation of our services during your research.
