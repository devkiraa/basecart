# Basecart Pricing & Trial Strategy

## 1. Core Pricing Model

Basecart operates on a **60-Day Full-Access Free Trial of the Growth Plan** followed by 3 subscription tiers and custom enterprise contracts. There is **no permanent free plan**.

| Tier | Price | Highlights | Marketing Email Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **60-Day Free Trial** | **₹0 for 60 Days** *(No CC required)* | Full Growth Plan access, unlimited products & orders, custom domain | **10 emails** *(Trial cap)* |
| **Starter** | **₹299 / month** | Essential storefront, core checkout, standard themes, basic analytics | **100 emails / month** |
| **Growth ⭐** | **₹799 / month** *(Recommended)* | Advanced AI product writer, full API access, priority support | **5,000 emails / month** |
| **Business** | **₹1,499 / month** | Multi-staff accounts, high-volume automation, custom integrations | **Unlimited** |
| **Enterprise** | **Custom** | Dedicated infrastructure, SLA guarantee, custom theme development | **Custom Limit** |

---

## 2. Free Trial & Lifecycle Rules

1. **Trial Experience & Protection Controls**:
   * All new signups automatically start on a **60-Day Free Trial with Growth Plan features**.
   * No credit card required at registration.
   * Full access to products, themes, Razorpay integration, custom domains, and AI description tools.
   * **Marketing Email Protection**: Transactional merchant emails (order confirmations) are always delivered. Promotional newsletter broadcasts and reminder campaigns are capped at **10 emails during the free trial** to protect domain reputation and prevent spam abuse while allowing full testing.

2. **Expiration Lifecycle**:
   * **Day 0 - 60**: Full access to storefront, merchant dashboard, and live order processing.
   * **Day 60 (Trial Expired)**: Storefront pauses (shows subscription notice to visitors). Dashboard remains accessible.
   * **Day 60 - 90**: Merchant retains full access to export catalog, orders, and customer data.
   * **Day 120**: Inactive stores scheduled for purging unless upgraded.

* **Button CTAs**: Replace generic "Start Free" with **"Start 60-Day Free Trial"** or **"Start Trial (Growth Plan)"**.
* **Value Messaging**: Emphasize "No credit card required • 60-day full access to Growth Plan • Cancel or upgrade anytime".
